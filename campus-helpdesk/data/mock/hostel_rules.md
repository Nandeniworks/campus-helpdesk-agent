# University Campus Hostel Rules and Regulations

## 1. General Conduct
* Resident students must maintain high standards of discipline, decorum, and mutual respect.
* Possession or consumption of alcoholic beverages, narcotics, and tobacco products on campus premises is strictly prohibited.
* Ragging in any form is a criminal offense under national law and university regulations.

## 2. Curfew and Entry Timings
* **Night Curfew**: The hostel main gates are locked strictly at **21:30 (9:30 PM)**.
* **Late Return**: Any entry between 21:30 and 22:30 requires prior written sanction from the Assistant Warden.
* **Night Out / Leave Passes**: Students intending to stay overnight outside the hostel must submit an online e-gate pass signed by their registered parent/guardian at least 24 hours in advance.

## 3. Visitors and Guest Policy
* **Visiting Hours**: Approved visitors may visit between **16:00 and 19:00** on weekdays and **10:00 to 18:00** on weekends/public holidays.
* Visitors must register at the main security post and are restricted to common reception lounges.
* No outside visitor or day-scholar student is permitted inside student residential rooms under any circumstances.
* Overnight stays for immediate family (parents) in university guest houses require advance reservation through the Chief Warden's Office.

## 4. Room Allocation and Facilities
* Electrical appliances such as immersion heaters, induction stoves, and high-wattage room heaters are prohibited in individual rooms.
* High-speed campus Wi-Fi is monitored for academic use; peer-to-peer torrenting is blocked.
* Rooms, corridors, and washrooms are inspected weekly by the hostel administration.
